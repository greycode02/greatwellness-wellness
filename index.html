What is Person-Centered Care?

Person-centered care is an approach that places the individual at the heart of their care. It recognizes that each person has unique preferences, values, and needs that should guide all healthcare decisions and interactions.

At GreatWellness Services, we don't just fill staffing positions—we match caregivers based on personality, communication style, and care philosophy to ensure meaningful connections and effective support.

Individualized Care

Tailored support that respects personal preferences, routines, and lifestyle choices.

Respectful Communication

Active listening and clear communication that honors each person's voice and choices.

Collaborative Partnership

Working together with individuals, families, and care teams to achieve shared goals.

Dignity & Respect

Treating every person with the utmost respect and preserving their dignity in all situations.
